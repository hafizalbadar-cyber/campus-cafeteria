How to Order

Simple steps to get your food quickly and seamlessly.

Home Menu Daily Deals How to Order Feedback

Step 1: Choose Your Items

Browse our menu or daily deals online or at the digital boards above the counter.

Step 2: Pay at Cash Counter

Place your order at Counter 1 or 2. We accept Cash, Debit Cards, and Mobile Payments (Easypaisa/JazzCash).

Step 3: Collect Token

Receive your printed order slip containing your order number/token.

Step 4: Pick Up Food

Wait for your token number to be displayed on the digital screen, then collect your order from Pickup Counter 3.

📌 Peak Hours & Guidelines

  • Peak Hours: 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM (Expect a 10-15 minute wait time).
  • Self-Service: Please return your tray to the return counter after finishing your meal.
  • Pre-Orders: Pre-ordering for group meetings can be done at the counter 1 hour in advance.
